samewhere. — Analytics notice

samewhere. uses GameAnalytics to understand how players progress through the
game. It sends gameplay milestones and cumulative active play time, session
data, a randomly generated player identifier stored in your browser, and
technical information such as browser, operating system and approximate
location derived from the connection's IP address.

Your game save and settings are stored in your browser. Clearing browser data
removes them and can reset the analytics identifier.
